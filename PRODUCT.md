# 产品定位 — 递归智能主页

> 当前目标：这是 noobli 的空间化个人主页，不是传统简历站。`data/` 目录中的经历、项目和 AI 思考只作为参考素材，用来校准真实背景与表达，不改变当前“递归智能界面宣言”的产品方向。

## Project

This site is a personal homepage for noobli, a former frontend engineer moving into AI-native product and interface thinking.

The page should not read like a conventional portfolio. It should feel like a spatial manifesto: a quiet, high-end interface that uses WebGL, scroll, inertia, and restrained copy to express a position about the AI era.

## Core Position

The interface is no longer for humans alone.

AI changes the conditions around software: workflows, tools, organizations, cognition, and eventually the shape of interfaces themselves. Making room for AI is not just adding AI features. It is rebuilding the room.

The homepage should express this stance through structure and interaction, not through long philosophical explanation.

## Identity

Noobli is positioned as:

- A former frontend engineer who understands the old interface stack from the inside.
- A builder moving toward AI-native systems, recursive workflows, and agentic tools.
- A person interested in the causal chain of the AI revolution: tool changes workflow, workflow changes organization, organization changes cognition, cognition changes the world.
- A designer-engineer who treats interface, motion, space, and performance as part of the same system.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- AI product teams, founders, researchers, and builders.
- People who care about the future of software interfaces.
- Visitors who should remember a point of view, not just a list of skills.

Secondary audience:

- Recruiters or collaborators who need to understand that the author can build refined frontend/WebGL experiences.
- Technical peers who can appreciate disciplined performance and interaction architecture.

## Brand Voice

The voice is calm, precise, and declarative.

It should avoid hype, startup slogans, long essays, and obvious AI buzzwords. Philosophy should appear as structure and implication. The page may use short English statements, supported by subtle Chinese notes later if needed.

Voice qualities:

- Architectural
- Recursive
- Quietly radical
- Systems-minded
- Premium, restrained, low-saturation

## Main Thesis

AI is not entering the old interface. It is bending it.

The page should communicate that old UI patterns were designed for human-only operation. As AI becomes a participant in work, the interface must reorganize around observability, feedback loops, self-iteration, and systems that can improve their own conditions.

## Narrative Arc

1. **Observation**
   The visitor observes the system. The system responds with subtle parallax and inertia.

2. **Causality**
   Scrolling reveals that every tool change creates a chain reaction: workflow, organization, cognition, infrastructure.

3. **Recursion**
   The WebGL object begins to behave like a feedback system. Output becomes input. The page implies self-iteration through motion and structure.

4. **Self-Reference**
   The site describes AI changing interfaces while it itself behaves like an AI-native interface. This should appear once, quietly.

5. **Reconstruction**
   Nodes, grid, and light reorganize. The old slab does not explode. It makes room. The final state is a rebuilt structure.

## Desired Visitor Impression

The visitor should leave thinking:

- This person has taste.
- This person understands frontend deeply, but is not trapped in old frontend identity.
- This person has a serious point of view about AI.
- This person can build interfaces that feel spatial, intelligent, and stable.

## Product Constraints

- Do not turn the site into a text-heavy philosophy essay.
- Do not turn physics into a game.
- Do not add multiple competing 3D focal objects.
- Do not change the existing obsidian and warm off-white identity without a separate design decision.
- Do not use bounce, elastic, or playful overshoot.
- Do not use Inter, Roboto, or system-ui for page text.
- Keep performance stable on mobile and desktop.

## Success Criteria

- The first viewport feels premium, spatial, and memorable within 3 seconds.
- The page can be understood without reading paragraphs of explanation.
- Scroll changes the meaning of the WebGL object, not only its position.
- Interaction expresses observation, inertia, causality, recursion, and reconstruction.
- The site remains smooth on modern laptops and acceptable on mobile with reduced effects.
- The implementation remains maintainable: one interaction loop, one WebGL scene, clear state ownership.
